## 🌟 Key Features

1. **University Major Catalog & Syllabi**: Complete official catalog coverage across Engineering, Sciences, Humanities, and Interdisciplinary programs, with deep dives into emerging fields:
   - **Artificial Intelligence** (`080717T`, 4 Years, Engineering)
   - **Embodied AI** (`140012TK`, Interdisciplinary)
   - **Brain-Computer Interfaces & Science** (`140013TK`, Life & Health Frontiers)
2. **Curriculum Prerequisite Topology**: Interactive directed graphs illustrating which foundation courses (e.g. Linear Algebra, Data Structures) are prerequisites for advanced topics.
3. **Admission Scoreline & Percentile Archives**: Historic province-level admission cutoff lines, rank percentiles, and annual recruitment plans.
4. **Civil Service Exam (考公) Matching**: Direct lookup connecting undergraduate major codes with national and provincial civil service position catalogs.
5. **Admission Strategy & Risk Balancer (冲稳保)**: Preference-based multi-tier sorting helping applicants balance stretch, realistic, and safety schools.

## 🇨🇳 中文介绍 · 知途 (Major)

### 为什么需要知途？
很多高中生与家长在高考填报志愿或大学转专业时，常常陷入“看专业名称顾名思义”的误区，往往在入学后才发现所学课程并不符合预期。

**知途（Major）坚持权威公开数据优先**，帮助求学者看清专业本质与长远发展：
1. **查专业（课程图谱与就业考研）**：
   - 详尽拆解专业学制、核心必修课及先修依赖拓扑图；
   - 追踪高校官方就业质量报告，呈现真实升学考研率与产业去向。
2. **查大学（章程、特色与学科评估）**：
   - 收录 800+ 高校招生章程、特色优势学科与国家级一流本科专业建设点。
3. **查投档线（历年分省位次）**：
   - 汇集分省分科类历年录取最高分、最低分与对应位次区间。
4. **考公专业匹配池**：
   - 一键查询本专业可报考的国家公务员与各省省考招录岗位池及历年报考热度。
5. **志愿优化与冲稳保策略**：
   - 基于考生成绩位次与个人城市、专业偏好，科学搭建梯度合理的志愿方案，防范退档风险。
